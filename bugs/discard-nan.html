WebGPU in Safari and Firefox: a fragment shader with two discards and a handful of loops leaves NaN in the colour attachment

Every row draws one triangle covering a 256×256 rgba16float attachment cleared to black, 20 frames, and reads the attachment back after each. The fragment shader writes one finite colour and discards some fragments, so every texel must be the clear value or a finite colour. The rows differ only in the fragment shader, shown below. NaN texels are painted magenta in the image of the worst frame.

The failing shape: two discards, the second inside an if; then an if whose body holds about nine or more small for loops. Take away either discard, un-nest the second one, move the discards after the loops, drop the branch around the loops, or use fewer loops, and the attachment is clean. Safari and Firefox both fail; Chrome on the same GPU is clean in every row.
